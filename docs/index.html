BlockPop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game BlockPop (package name com.blockpop.puzzlegame), developed by SS Enterprises ("we", "us"), handles information. BlockPop is an offline block puzzle game. We built it so that it does not collect any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. BlockPop has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress only on your own device. This includes your best score, adventure levels, daily streak, medals, and sound settings. This data never leaves your phone, and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback while you play.
  • Internet / network state: these are included by default by the app framework. The game does not send any of your data over the internet.

Spoken praise words such as "Excellent!" use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app does not use the microphone.

4. Third-party services

BlockPop does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. When you download the app, Google Play may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's privacy

BlockPop is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 or any other age.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through github.com/websachin9728/puzzle/issues.
